About Me

I am a Rutgers University computer science student with strong interests in systems software, scalable backend architecture, and practical AI-assisted products. I like solving technical problems that require both low-level reasoning and high-level platform design.

Technical Background

My work spans C systems programming, Python service development, and TypeScript-based web applications. Across these environments, I focus on building maintainable software with clean interfaces, predictable performance, and measurable operational behavior.

I am especially interested in areas where concurrency, data movement, and runtime scheduling decisions influence user-facing performance. That includes thread models, queueing behavior, replication workflows, and network protocol tradeoffs.

Core Interests

  • Distributed systems design, RPC interfaces, and consistency models.
  • Data platform engineering: ingestion, persistence, and stream processing.
  • Systems-level performance tuning through profiling and benchmark-driven iteration.
  • Dev tooling and developer experience improvements through automation.
  • Applied AI integrations that are production-aware and testable.

Academic + Build Philosophy

I value projects that are technically rigorous and practically useful. When I design systems, I explicitly define assumptions around latency, reliability, and scalability targets before implementation. I then treat observability and documentation as first-class features, not afterthoughts.

I also prefer iterative architecture: ship an understandable baseline, verify behavior under realistic load, and improve bottlenecks with clear evidence. This mindset helps me move quickly while still preserving engineering quality.

What I Want to Keep Building

Going forward, I want to work on infrastructure-heavy engineering teams where I can contribute across the full technical lifecycle: design, implementation, performance validation, and handoff. My strongest fit is in environments that value rigorous systems thinking and practical software delivery at scale.
